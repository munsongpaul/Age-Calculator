How to Use the Age Calculator

Simply enter your birth date into the tool to calculate your exact age in years, months, and days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my birth date data stored on a server?
A: No, this calculation is performed entirely locally in your web browser. Your data is private.

Q: Is this tool free to use?
A: Yes, this is a free, privacy-first utility tool provided for your convenience.
